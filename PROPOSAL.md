# Capstone Proposal

## Audience

This demo is for small outpatient clinics that need one place to coordinate patient appointment requests, clinician schedules, invoices, and pharmacy stock.

## Questions the charts answer

1. **Is the practice growing?** A monthly chart of newly registered patients helps the clinic owner see whether patient demand is increasing before committing to added clinic hours.
2. **What share of appointments are no-shows?** A monthly appointment-outcome donut compares completed visits with no-shows so the team can decide whether reminders or follow-up are needed.

## Scope and limits

The demo models appointment and pharmacy workflows with sample data. It is not a clinical record system, does not yet enforce Supabase Row Level Security for app queries, and must not store real patient information until security and privacy requirements are reviewed.
